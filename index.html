// FAQ

Questions clients ask

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. DataPulse is based in Islamabad and works remotely with clients worldwide. Calls are scheduled to fit your time zone, and updates are shared in writing every week.

How is pricing decided?

After a free discovery call, you receive a written proposal with a fixed price, scope and timeline. Larger projects are billed by milestone, so you only pay as work is delivered.

Who owns the code and the models?

You do. On final payment, all source code, trained models and documentation are handed over to you in full.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data is used only for your project, never shared, and an NDA can be signed before you share anything sensitive.

What if I don't know exactly what I need?

That's what the discovery call is for. Tell me the business problem — slow reporting, too many support messages, unclear sales trends — and I'll recommend the simplest solution that solves it.
